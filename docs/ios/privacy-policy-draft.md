## On your device

The iOS app keeps an encrypted copy of your affirmations, goals, journal entries and rituals so you can
read them offline, plus any journal entry written offline until it uploads. The encryption key and your
sign-in tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain. Reminder notifications are scheduled on your device.
Signing out or deleting the app removes this data from the device.

## AI features

AI responses are generated automatically and may be inaccurate. Manifest is a wellness and
self-reflection tool. **It is not medical or mental-health treatment** and is not a substitute for a
professional. If you mention self-harm, the app shows crisis resources (in the U.S., call or text 988)
instead of an AI reply. You can report any AI response from inside the app. You can withdraw AI consent
at any time in Settings; AI features then stop working until you agree again.

## SMS reminders

SMS reminders are off unless you enable them and check the consent box. Message frequency: up to one
message per day. Message and data rates may apply. Reply **STOP** to cancel or **HELP** for help.
Consent is not a condition of using the app.
