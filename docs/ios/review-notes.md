**Notes**

Manifest is a manifestation and self-reflection wellness app by Joshua Barone (individual developer).
Users set goals, write and generate affirmations, journal with a mood, build vision boards (including
AI-generated images), talk with an AI coach, and keep morning/evening rituals and streaks.

- Sign in: Sign in with Apple or email/password. (A Replit login option exists only on the website.)
- Native features: on-device reminder notifications (Settings → Reminders), haptics when saving or
  completing, an offline read cache with queued journal entries, the iOS Share sheet on affirmations,
  native splash/status bar.
- AI: before any AI feature runs, a one-time consent screen explains that the user's text is sent to
  OpenAI. All AI inputs and outputs pass OpenAI moderation. Every AI response has a "Report" control.
  If a user mentions self-harm, the app shows crisis resources (988 etc.) instead of an AI reply.
- Health: the app shows "not medical or mental-health treatment" during onboarding and in Settings.
  It makes no medical claims.
- Account deletion: Settings → Account → Delete account (type DELETE to confirm). This permanently deletes
  the account and all data and revokes Sign in with Apple.
- SMS reminders are optional, need explicit consent, and honor STOP/HELP.
- Operator tools (a training dashboard for the AI persona) are restricted to admin accounts and are not
  visible to the demo account or any consumer.
- No in-app purchases, ads or tracking.
