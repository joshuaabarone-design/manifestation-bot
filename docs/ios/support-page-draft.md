## Common questions

**How do I sign in?**
Use Sign in with Apple, or an email and password. If you used "Hide My Email" with Apple, your account
uses Apple's relay address.

**I forgot my password.**
[PLACEHOLDER: there is no self-service reset yet. Email support from your account's address and I'll help.]

**How do reminders work?**
Settings → Reminders. In the iPhone app, reminders are notifications scheduled on your device at the time
you choose (allow notifications when asked). You can also choose SMS reminders, which need your phone number
and your consent. Reply STOP to any text to stop them, HELP for help.

**Does the app work offline?**
You can read your affirmations, goals, journal and rituals offline. Journal entries you write offline are
saved and uploaded when you're back online. AI features need a connection.

**What happens to what I type into AI features?**
It is sent to OpenAI to generate a response, only after you agree on the AI consent screen. You can turn
this off in Settings. See the [Privacy Policy]([PLACEHOLDER: privacy policy URL]).

**An AI response was wrong or upsetting.**
Tap "Report" under the response. Reports are reviewed. Manifest is not medical or mental-health treatment.

**I need help right now.**
If you are in the U.S., call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or text HOME to 741741.
Elsewhere, visit [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com). In an emergency call your local
emergency number.

**How do I delete my account?**
Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all your data.
